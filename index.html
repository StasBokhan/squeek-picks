The small print

The night

A night runs from 12:00 to 12:00 UTC, the same day the Squeekchain API uses. Picks for a night close when it starts, so you pick the next night while you watch this one. You can change them until then.

Points

  • Distance: 30, 20, 10 or 5 points within 0.25, 0.5, 1 or 2 km. The closest guess of the night gets 10 more.
  • Busiest 3 hours: 20 for the right window, 5 for the one next to it.
  • Blocks: 15 for calling more or fewer than the night before.
  • A market with a data gap, a tie or a night off doesn't count: no points either way.

Results

Distance and the busiest hours come from closed, accepted rounds, so they settle once the night's data is in. Blocks come from blocks settled on Ethereum, counted by when they settled.

The pot

  • Optional, in Sepolia test ETH, which has no value. A ticket uses your three picks; up to 5 tickets a wallet a night.
  • The night's top 3 tickets split the pot 50/30/20 (62.5/37.5 with two). Ties go to the closer distance, then the earlier ticket.
  • Winnings are sent automatically within the hour. With one ticket or no result the night is voided and you take your ticket back yourself.
  • Nobody can withdraw the pot: the contract only pays a night's tickets, or refunds them. It was audited by the IMD swarm; version 3 fixes the three low findings.

Streaming him?

Add squeekpicks.xyz/overlay as a Browser source in OBS (1600 × 140). Add ?theme=light for a paper strip.
