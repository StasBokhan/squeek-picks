How it works

The night

A night runs from 12:00 to 12:00 UTC, the same day the Squeekchain API uses. Picks for a night close the moment it starts, so you pick the next night while you watch the current one. You can change your picks until then.

Points

  • Distance: 30, 20, 10 or 5 points within 0.25, 0.5, 1 or 2 km. Closest guess of the night gets 10 more.
  • Peak window: 20 for the right 3 hours, 5 for the window next to it.
  • Blocks: 15 for calling more or fewer correctly.

Results

Distance and peak come from closed, accepted rounds, so they settle once the night's data is in. Blocks come from blocks settled on Ethereum, counted by when they settled. A pick with a data gap, a tie or a night off is void: no points either way.

The pot

Every ticket costs the same. When the night's results are in, the top 3 tickets by that night's points split the pot 50/30/20, or 62.5/37.5 with two tickets. Ties go to the closer distance guess, then the earlier ticket. A bot pays out every hour. With one ticket or no result, the night is voided and tickets are refunded. If a night isn't paid out within 3 days, everyone can take their ticket back.

Nothing here can make him run, and nothing should. He's never woken up to mine, and quiet nights are part of the game. If the operator's device reports wrong data, results follow it, which is one more reason this is for points only.
